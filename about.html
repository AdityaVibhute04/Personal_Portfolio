About the archive

A long-term home for engineering work.

AV
B.Tech + M.TechEnergy Science & EngineeringIIT Bombay · 2026

This website is a living archive of work completed during the Energy Science and Engineering Dual Degree at IIT Bombay. It is designed to be useful both as a public engineering portfolio and as a searchable technical reference years later.

Project pages do not reproduce reports page-for-page. They extract the engineering story: the problem, methodology, key decisions, results, limitations and—where the source supports it—individual contribution.

Evidence over exaggerationSimulation, design estimates, physical prototypes and field observations are labelled distinctly.
Small work still has a placeAssignments and field visits are preserved under course collections instead of being inflated into “major projects.”
Built to growProjects can be filtered by domain while the archive remains chronological by academic year and course.
Original sources retainedSubmitted reports and selected source archives remain accessible where appropriate, with sensitive records kept out of the public build.
