About the archive

A long-term home for engineering coursework.

AV

This website is designed as a living archive of projects completed through the Department of Energy Science and Engineering at IIT Bombay.

The goal is not to reproduce academic reports page-for-page. Each project is converted into a concise engineering narrative: the problem, the approach, the important technical decisions, the results, limitations and — where the source report supports it — the work personally contributed.

Credible by defaultTeam work, individual work, design estimates and experimentally demonstrated results are kept distinct.
Useful years laterOriginal reports stay attached to each page, so the portfolio doubles as a searchable personal knowledge base.
Built to growNew projects can be added without changing the visual system or navigation structure.
